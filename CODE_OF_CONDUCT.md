# Code of Conduct

We want MeetScribe to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Do: be kind and patient, give and accept constructive feedback, assume good faith, credit others' work.

Don't: harass, insult, discriminate, publish others' private information (including meeting content), or troll.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and PRs, and ban people who violate this code. Report problems privately via a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/AmineChr54/MeetScribe/security/advisories/new) (mark it as conduct-related) or by contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
